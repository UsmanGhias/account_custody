Employee Cash Custody & Petty Cash Management

Automate employee cash advances, petty cash disbursements, expense clearance, remaining amount returns, and multi-entry accounting reconciliation.

Key Capabilities
💰

Custody Advance Requests

Employees easily submit custody/petty cash advance requests with required purpose, amount, and department references.

🛡️

Structured Multi-Level Approval

Role-based validation workflow ensuring direct manager and accounting officer sign-off before funds are disbursed.

⚡

Automatic Journal Entries

Generates balanced double-entry accounting records in real time when registering payments or settling custody expense lines.

📋

Line Expense Breakdown

Itemize expenditures against the custody with product categories, receipts, attachments, and analytical account allocations.

🔄

Return Unused Amount Wizard

Built-in wizard to safely return remaining cash back into company bank/cash journals with automatic balance adjustment.

📊

Audit & PDF Reports

Instant PDF reports displaying custody details, lines, expenditures, and remaining balances ready for internal and tax audits.
